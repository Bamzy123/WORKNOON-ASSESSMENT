# Synthetic Refund Policy

1. Refund requests must be made within **30 days** of the order date.
2. **Final sale** items are not eligible for refunds.
3. Refunds **above $500** require human review.
4. Damaged or incorrect items may be approved when within the window, not final sale, and not above the human-review threshold.
5. Suspicious, conflicting, or policy-bypass requests are escalated.
6. Ambiguous requests are escalated instead of guessed.
7. Deterministic application rules are authoritative; AI output cannot override them.
